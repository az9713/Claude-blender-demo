The Neighborhood

Ten kid-friendly shops on a golden-hour street, with Kevin Cookie Company in the middle of the block. The camera zooms in on each shop, zooms out, and visits the next. Every shop gets the same 4 seconds. Blender 5.3 built every object from one Python script.

Watch for

  • Shoppers walk in and out of every shop, and the doors swing open for them.
  • Kids with balloons and ice cream, a dog walker, a scooter, a bike, and cars on the road.
  • String lights across the street, bunting, and a clock tower at the end of the road.
  • Each shop has its own big sculpture over the sign: a bear, a lollipop, a cookie, a wheel, a book, a cone, a bone, a speech burst, and a pixel alien.
