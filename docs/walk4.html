Known problems

  • The light-shaft haze is nearly invisible.
  • Some sound effects are quiet against the music.
  • A few reactions are small, for example the dog at 41.0 s.
  • The dancers overlap their partners for a few frames at 25.4 s to 26.1 s and at about 46 s.
